Yanming Xiu 修彦名

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Intelligent Interactive Internet of Things (I³T) Lab at Duke University, advised by Prof. Maria Gorlatova.

My research focuses on safe and trustworthy augmented reality, scene understanding, and vision-language models. I develop systems and benchmarks for detecting task-detrimental and deceptive virtual content, and for assessing and improving AR content quality.
